## Strict checks requested

- **M06 "ahead" and repetition.** The effect (above-null decoding) repeats in all three subjects, and Extended Data Fig. 9 ablations support the role of the encoding model. The three named priors were read and do not have LM-generated candidates. However, [5] has the encoding-model scorer, so the review's "earliest among 18" sentence is false as written.
- **Earlier encoding-model scoring.** In the ledger, Wehbe 2014 (fixed two-way candidates, fMRI, reading) and pmc-5481359 (2017, zero-shot encoding model) already used encoding-model scoring. Outside the wiki: Kay 2008 (identification), Mitchell 2008 (nouns, fMRI, language), Naselaris 2009 and Nishimoto 2011 (Bayesian decoding over an empirical prior). All are absent from the wiki, and the target cites Naselaris and Nishimoto itself. Pereira 2018 is also absent; it decodes toward semantic vectors and ranks candidates, so it is not encoding-model scoring.
- **Does "ahead" survive?** Yes, but only narrowed to what the authors claim: a generative LM prior with beam search under an encoding-model likelihood, in non-invasive language decoding. I know of no earlier non-invasive language study with that design. The nearest generative analogue is invasive (Herff 2015 ECoG "Brain-to-Text", phone likelihoods with an LM in Viterbi decoding), and it is also absent from the wiki. Restate the claim, name the Bayesian-decoder lineage, and say that the novelty is the authors' own stated claim. As written, the claim and its support sentence do not hold.
- **"Behind" verdicts.** None are given, so the rule does not apply.
- **Protocol, not results.** The verdicts follow the design. One exception: the M06 state-of-the-art line, "direct mapping has since overtaken the search", judges a later result and misnames the design that changed (claim 8).
- **Counting bases.** The numbers agree across text, table and Appendix (251/335, 112/335, 45/132, 18/335), and the table counts are design counts. However, the table cells give no denominators ("pattern match 251", "112", "45"). The M07 text sentence does not point to the Appendix. The M06 usual approach is said to describe 2022, but 251/335 counts the whole ledger, which is mostly 2023–2026 (about 90 of the 395 candidate folders are dated 2022 or earlier), so it is not the usual approach at the time. For M07, slot-filled 119 is smaller than pattern match 335, and the tier names do not explain this.
- **Chronology against 2022-09.** The labels are right: [4] 2024-03, [7] 2026, [8] 2025 and [9] 2026 are [later]; [2] 2020, [3] 2014, [5] 2014 and [6] 2021 are prior. No later work is treated as prior. Défossez et al. (arXiv 2208.12266, 2022-08, in the wiki) would be [concurrent] and is correctly not used as a prior.
- **Quotes and numbers.** All numbers match their sources. Two quotations fail: the "cooperation" phrase (claim 16) and the code-only parameters (claim 24).
