## Judgement on the points checked strictly

- **Is "ahead" about protocol or result?** About protocol. The paper paired covert and overt discrimination of speech units (vowels and consonant pairs) within the same patients on clinical ECoG. No earlier wiki paper does that, and the read priors (Deng 2010, Guenther 2009, Kellis 2010) were all read in full and differ (EEG rhythm, attempted, overt). The result is modest and selection-inflated: 12.5 points over chance at the best electrode, and the covert effect survives a correction for electrode selection in only 2/6 and 1/6 patients (inference, row 4). The review treats 5/6 and 4/6 as the repetition required for `ahead` while arguing elsewhere that these values are upper bounds. That is internally inconsistent. Keep `ahead`, state that it is a protocol verdict, and report the corrected repetition.
- **Deciding priors read:** yes, all three plus Leuthardt are in `read_fulltext` with role prior and quotes. Guenther was missed by the M04 pattern (known-item recall 0.5) but was read.
- **Earlier wiki work:** none found before 13 Jul 2011 that decodes imagined or covert speech from intracranial signals, except the concurrent Leuthardt (Apr 2011, closed-loop imagined phoneme vs rest) and Brumberg 2011 (May 2011, attempted).
- **"Behind":** not claimed. M06 could not be `behind` because the base has fewer than 3 comparable earlier ledger papers (effectively Guenther/Brumberg plus Kellis), so `standard` is correct under the rule.
- **M04 10 of 39:** it counts pre-2011 pattern-match hits whose full text mentions scalp-EEG terms. It does not count EEG imagined-speech decoders or "no overt pairing". Only 1 of the 10 is such a decoder.
