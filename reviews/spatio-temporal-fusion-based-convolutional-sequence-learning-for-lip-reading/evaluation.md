## Required fixes, by impact

High
- H1. Delete or rewrite the second "Where the claims outrun the evidence" bullet (claim 16). "Not comparable" in §4.3 describes the abandoned ResNet-50 two-stage run without MV-LRS(w). "Comparable" describes the final ResNet-18 end-to-end model. Also reconsider the Verdict sentence on "trail": the LM-free gaps of 1.9 and 0.2 points with about half the training video (863 h against 1,637.4 h, target Table 4) are the strongest confound. Read that way, they support the authors' "comparable with less data" wording rather than refuting it. State the data difference next to the 49.8/59.9 comparison.
- H2. SyncVSR line (M06): remove "LRW data only". State "157 h of LRW video, with vq-wav2vec audio tokens pretrained on outside audio as targets". Add that this is a whole-system result, not a spatial-reduction comparison; the audit already says so. If an LRW result on the reduction step itself is not available, say so plainly.

Medium
- M1. M12 and Appendix: the V2P ± intervals are bootstrap estimates (arXiv 1807.05162, Table 1 caption), not run spread. Change "4 of 5 ... no run spread" to 5 of 5, and "run spread 1" to 0 (bootstrap intervals 1). Fix the table cell and `slots.json` `matched` to match.
- M2. Fig. 4 sentence (claim 12): separate the two settings. Widths above 100 give the same accuracy only when a globally trained model is restricted at test time. Training with local attention is 20% faster with "little drop in accuracy", and the width is not given.
- M3. USR line: add 223 h labelled plus 1,759 h unlabelled. Mention Auto-AVSR's 14.6% (with LM, 3,448 h) from the same Table 6 if the line stays "state of the art on LRS2".
- M4. Tables 1/2 TM-seq2seq question: cite the §4.2 detail (two extra 1×1 convolutions before GAP in Table 2) and note that it cannot explain why the TM-CTC and Conv-seq2seq rows stay identical.

Low
- L1. LinProVSR: replace "split type not named" with "24,750/8,250 train/test split (its Table 2), not the 255-per-speaker split".
- L2. LP Conformer: write "unreleased set mined from public YouTube videos, no external LM". Correct the ICASSP title to "Conformer is All You Need for Visual Speech Recognition" and add doi:10.1109/ICASSP48485.2024.10446532.
- L3. References: [4] TPAMI vol. 44(12), pp. 8717-8727, 2022 (online 2018). [3] CVIU 176-177, pp. 22-32, doi:10.1016/j.cviu.2018.10.003.
- L4. FC-15 timing: note that the 3.4 days (3.5 in the text) and 13 days exclude two weeks of front-end training.
- L5. GRID: write "the LipNet random split, 255 test utterances per speaker (§4.1)". Mark "overlapped-speaker" as LipNet's term.
- L6. Population (claim 4 and the Appendix): sampling the prior list of 121 found one non-decoder, doi-10.1109-access.2017.2761539 (cross audio-visual matching, a synchronisation/verification task). It also found a general action-recognition paper with a VSR application (arXiv 1503.06699). And it found Bear's thesis and overview (arXiv 1710.01288, 1710.01351), which overlap her ICASSP and FAAVSP papers in the same list. The denominator also contains about 30 HMM-era AVSR fusion papers. Label it "lip-reading, VSR and AVSR decoders". None of these rows is among the 13 that state a reduction, so no conclusion changes. Dating is sound: target first public 2019-10 (ICCV, no arXiv); prior before 2019-04; [concurrent] 2019-04 to 2020-04; every cited prior is from 2018-11 or earlier, and every state-of-the-art work is labelled [later].
- L7. Same-group prior: Cheng, Wang & Liew 2018 (target ref. 9) is a dual-task CNN that recognises the prompt text as well as the speaker. "Speaker-authentication study" undersells its content-recognition branch. This is unverified: the paper is not in the wiki.
- L8. Wiki side (not the draft): `papers/cvf-zhang19-stfcsl/source/manifest.json` has the wrong DOI (00300, which is DAGMapper). Report it to the wiki owner.

## What holds

The Stafylakis precedents ([2], [3]) are verified in both papers and are uncited by the target. The Table 1 LRW arithmetic error (2.4 against 3.4) is a genuine internal inconsistency, and so is the Tables 1/2 TM-seq2seq mismatch. The "+ extLM" provenance of the target's Table 3 rows is verified. The LRW counterexample (15.67% without word boundaries) is like-for-like. The LCANet and V2P omissions are verified with their conditions. The DOI and pages are correct. The text is in English only, has no model names and no process disclaimers, and correctly credits the authors' own caveats (§4.2 inconsistency, Table 4 data differences), apart from H1.
