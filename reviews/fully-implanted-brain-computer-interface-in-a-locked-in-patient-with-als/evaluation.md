## What known-item recall 0.4, with the target outside the pattern, does to the counts

- The pattern population leaves out the whole skin-closed ECoG home-use lineage the review is about: the target, its 2024 follow-up and the 2026 night study. It also leaves out the later skin-closed Stentrode home study. The 50 "fully implanted" and 19 "independent use" matches therefore describe mainly intracortical speech papers and the reviews that mention such systems, not the designs being compared.
- "Upper bound" is the wrong label. The counts are inflated by mentions, reviews and duplicates, and deflated by false negatives at 0.6 recall or worse. The direction of the error is unknown, so each count should be called a pattern count of unknown bias.
- The pre-2016 bins (10, 1, 1; 10, 1, 0; 9) are the most exposed. Known pre-2016 implanted communication work is absent from the wiki or from the pattern: Kennedy 1998/2000, and BrainGate home-session typing such as Jarosiewicz et al. 2015 (Sci Transl Med; not in the wiki, unverified). So "one lineage" and "none predates" are facts about this ledger, not about the field. Every absence sentence must say "in the ledger" and name a read prior.
- The verdicts survive. All are `standard`, and none depends on these counts. The novelty framing in the Verdict and the "usual approach" cells do depend on them.

## Verdict rules

- No `ahead` or `behind` verdicts. `standard` for the two M13 departures is correct under the rules: there is no repetition (one participant), and for the device a prior exists. Both verdicts judge the protocol. The rate comparison with [4] is a result comparison placed in the Verdict, not a verdict, but its condition is wrong (row 9).
- No component should be `behind`. Fewer than 3 earlier comparable ledger papers exist for any of the slots.

## Required fixes, ordered by impact

1. **Rate comparison (Verdict).** "55 times below" sets a peak by a spinal-cord-injury participant (110 cpm, T18) against the target's average-equivalent rate. Either compare with the ALS participant's peak (47 cpm, about 24×, inference) with the condition stated, or drop the factor. Name the skin-closed comparator, Oxley 2021: 13.81 correct characters per minute, 2 ALS users, unsupervised home use.
2. **Deciding prior for the skin-closed link.** The closest prior is Kennedy et al. 2000 (telemetric neurotrophic electrode, spelling by a locked-in user), which [2] cites as its ref. 32 and whose 1998 predecessor the target cites as its ref. 48. Guenther 2009 is not the closest prior. Name it as the deciding prior, or state that it was not read, and drop "differs in task" against the lineage.
3. **State of the art and "needs next" for M13.** Add Oxley 2021 (wiki `pmc-7848062-neurointerv`; fully implanted wireless Stentrode, 2 users, unsupervised home use). Revise "the skin-closed system in several users" accordingly. Its sentence calling the target "the only report of an unsupervised, ambulatory implantable BCI" is a better anchor for the independence claim than the pattern count.
4. **Absence claim on independent use.** Two of the 19 matches have unresolved dates. There is no full-text pattern or recall sample, and no prior was read. Restate it as a ledger fact with the 17 + 2 split, limit it to implanted systems, and name EEG independent home use (Sellers et al. 2010) as the boundary.
5. **Table counting basis.** Replace the M13 and M12 "usual approach" cells with design counts on one stated denominator, or label them as all-year mention counts. Show the pre-2016 numbers (10 of 103; 9 of 169), since these, not the all-year totals, describe what was usual for a 2016 paper. Reconcile them with the Appendix's 216/296 slot-filled design, or remove that line, and explain the 296 denominator.
6. **Night study [9].** Say that it is the same participant and system. This turns an analogy into direct evidence on the target's false-click rate.
7. **Appendix chronology and recall.** Relabel the bins "index year", not "first public". Mark the single fully-implanted and single home-use matches as [7] [concurrent]. Correct the recall line: [6] is in the population as `doi-10.1109-tbme.2021.3069119`, so recall is 0.6. Remove version duplicates from the 382, or state them.
8. **M12 state of the art.** Replace the 50-word accuracy with a work that reports a floor for click selection, such as false clicks per hour (e.g. [9]) or a chance level from the scan design, or say that no such work was found.
9. **"Same implant" [3].** The transmitter was replaced in 2020. Write "the same electrodes, transmitter replaced in 2020".
10. **"Not shown" (M12).** Limit the claim to the main text, or check Fig. S4 and the Supplementary Appendix before stating it.
11. **Minor.** Change "almost all from 2017 or later" to "most". The audit file `M13-wireless-implanted-speech-bci.audit.json` keeps stale `tally_<slug>` fields (state of the art = the target, timing "later"; recall 1.0) that contradict `known_item_recall_<slug>` (0.4). The review text is not affected.

What holds: all target-paper numbers and quotes match the source, the [later] and [concurrent] labels are right, the conservative `standard` verdicts follow the rules, and the inferences are labelled.

## Scores

- Procedure: 5/10. The target and the comparator quotes were checked carefully. But the absence claim was made without the full-text pattern, recall sample or a read prior. The deciding prior for the skin-closed link is not the closest one. A skin-closed multi-user comparator already in the wiki was missed. The recall line and the chronology bins are mislabelled.
- Final text: 6/10. The positions on the target are accurate and mostly well scoped. The Verdict carries one wrong-condition comparison (55×), and the M13 state of the art, table basis and "needs next" lines are incomplete or misleading.
