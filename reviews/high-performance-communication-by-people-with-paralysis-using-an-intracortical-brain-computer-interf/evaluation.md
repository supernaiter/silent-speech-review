## Required fixes, ordered by impact

1. **High: [3] is [concurrent] and is used as prior work (Verdict; M06).** Its DOI is a March 2017 registration; the target was published on 21 Feb 2017 and does not cite it. Label it [concurrent] and stop using it as evidence that the pairing was inherited. The prior human Kalman-plus-LDA-click work is Kim et al. 2011 (IEEE TNSRE 19:193–203), which the target cites in Fig. 3—figure supplement 1 as "the previous best discrete selection algorithm". Bacher et al. 2015 (Table 1, "CLC+LDA") is also prior. Relabel [2] from "date unresolved" to prior, because the target cites it (as 2016).
2. **High: the usual-approach column and the verdicts disagree, and the time base is not stated (M04, M06, M07, M12).** The table gives the field's usual approach as recurrent-network decoder, language model and imagined or inner speech. These come almost entirely from 2019–2026 records, yet every verdict is `standard` with no reason given for M04 and M07. State that the usual approach is judged at the target's time, and give that basis. Bear in mind that the audits' prior-period basis is weak: 3 records, none a proper human cursor-click text-entry prior, and all M12 metrics and M07 designs at 0. The defensible basis is the target's Table 1 (Kim 2011, Bacher 2015, Jarosiewicz 2015), cited as such. Otherwise call the slot `standard` because "the difference is not supported well enough to call", and say so.
3. **Medium: the population label is wrong (M06, M07, M12 text: "33 intracortical text-entry studies").** About 10 of the 33 are reviews or commentaries. One is a noninvasive MEG/EEG typing study and one is a speech-synthesizer study. Write "33 ledger records (pattern match, including reviews; upper bound)". Note that M07's completion count includes the target, which only excludes completion.
4. **Medium: the M06 Appendix prior-period line overstates.** "Kalman-family 3, click 1" comes from Brumberg 2009 (a synthesizer), a monkey dual-state cursor study and the concurrent [3]. Either name these three studies or drop the line as support for `standard`.
5. **Medium: an absence claim lacks its read prior (M06: "The only dwell-versus-click contrast is in monkeys … [2]").** It names no Appendix count, and the deciding prior, Kao et al. 2016 (IEEE TBME, doi:10.1109/TBME.2016.2582691), was not read. That is the HMM-click source paper and probably contains its own HMM-versus-dwell monkey comparison (unverified). Read it or drop "only". Also acknowledge the target's cross-study HMM-versus-Kim-2011 click comparison (Fig. 3—figure supplement 1) next to "No experiment isolates the HMM click from dwell selection".
6. **Medium: fairness in "Where the claims outrun the evidence", T7 bullet.** The quoted phrase is not verbatim; quote "all three participants achieved the highest BCI communication rates …" (§Discussion, Summary). The authors themselves disclose that T7 had no HMM, and they credit T7's result to continuous control. Reword along these lines: "As the authors note, the HMM click cannot account for T7's record; T7's gain comes from the ReFIT-KF cursor alone (Table 3; Discussion)."
7. **Medium: fairness in the experience bullet.** Report the authors' counter-argument ("similar range of experience … (Table 2)") before criticising it. In Table 2 the comparator participants had comparable or longer experience (Bacher S3 4.3 years; Jarosiewicz 2015 average 2 years). That weakens the learning confound, which should then be stated as weak or dropped. Fix the claim-table quote for "1.5 years", which currently cites T5's "9 prior sessions".
8. **Medium: the M04 state of the art is incomplete.** Name the faster speech decoders by rate and error: Willett et al. 2023, 62 wpm; Metzger et al. 2023, median 78 wpm at 25% median WER. Then say that 110 cpm at 1.6% WER [5] is the best low-error hand-based rate.
9. **Low: peak against mean.** The 2.8× (110 cpm, T18 peak) and 2.3× (90 cpm plateau) ratios are set against T5's session mean of 39.2 ccpm. Say so. Tie 1.6% WER to the Abstract's condition, since Results give T18 WER below 7% across blocks.
10. **Low: the spike-only bullet.** Aim it at the authors' comparative use of the T5-over-T6 result to rule out HF-LFP and EMG, not at the existence claim, which T5 supports.
11. **Low: a missed internal inconsistency in the target.** Fig. 3—figure supplement 1(b) is labelled "participant T7 … over 4 days" but reports T5. Add it to the grid-days bullet; it supports Results' 4 days over Fig. 3b's 2 days.
12. **Low: the multiple-comparison bullet has no consequence.** Three tests at p<0.01 survive Bonferroni (inference). Drop it or say why it matters.
13. **Low: the Appendix recall figures use different bases.** M04 reports this review's seed (0 of 1); M06 and M12 report "3 of 3" from seeds that are not this target's citations. Use one basis.
14. **Low: a confound not named.** The comparator participants were further from implantation: Bacher S3 had 4.3 years of BCI experience by Table 2, while T6 and T7 were 1.5 years post-implant (Discussion). Array age and signal quality differ between studies, so name this as part of the cross-study starting point (the S3 implant age is unverified here).

What holds: every number in the review that traces to a target or comparator quote matches its source. The (inference) arithmetic is right. Chronology labels for [4], [5] and [6] are right. The central position, that the record holds as performance but not as component evidence because the gains are cross-study and cross-task, is supported by Table 1 and Results.

The deciding prior for the HMM click (Kao et al. 2016) was not read. The prior-period audit basis was not inspected. The populations mix reviews and noninvasive work under an "intracortical" label.
