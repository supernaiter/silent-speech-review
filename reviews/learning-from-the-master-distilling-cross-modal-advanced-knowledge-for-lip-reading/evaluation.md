## Required fixes, by impact

**High**
1. Population (Appendix and every "of 221"). The tools/pop.py filter does not do what the Appendix says. The title-only exclusion regex (survey, perception, ...) misses most human-perception and neuroimaging titles. The "ledger names a model" regex accepts "network", "attention" or "decoder" in any sense, e.g. "cortical network". Versions are grouped only by ledger key, so the same study under different keys counts twice or three times. Remove the perception, neuroimaging, education and non-decoder rows (about 48) and merge the duplicates (about 10). Then restate the denominators: roughly 165 distinct prior decoders. Do this either by an explicit exclusion list or by a modality/task flag. The numerators for M02 (4; 0) and M03 (9) and the LRS2 mention counts do not change, but the LRW mention counts do.
2. M02 novelty (Verdict, M02, Appendix). Define the deciding design precisely: a teacher updated through the student's task loss after a look-ahead update, on held-out labelled data. Scope "new" to lip-reading and VSR decoders. Name Meta Pseudo Labels (Pham et al., 2020, arXiv 2003.10580) as the general-ML precedent with the same mechanism, and Learning to Teach (Fan et al., 2018) as a further one. Say that Xiao et al. [7] and DML-style mutual learning update the teacher from the student by a distillation loss, not by the student's task loss. Keep "standard". The rule for "ahead" is not met: the only isolating comparison is Table 4, on LRS2 with one value per row, and the master-vs-teacher result is not repeated on LRW or LRS3.
3. Verdict, M12 sentence. Replace "holds only against no-LM rows [2] [3] [4]". Suggested: "holds only against public-data, no-LM sentence rows; on LRW, born-again distillation (87.9% top-1 [4]) beats Ours-TM, and on LRS3 V2P (55.1%, 5-gram LM, 3,886 h non-public [3]) does."
4. M12 state of the art, LRS3. Either state the condition ("semi-supervised, public data: USR 2.0 17.6%") or cite LP Conformer at 12.8% (100,000 h, no LM, as listed in USR 2.0's table). For LRS2, add Auto-AVSR at 14.6% with an LM (3,448 h) next to USR's 16.0% without one.

**Medium**
5. M03 count. "6 state a length order" should be 8 of 9 explicit (9 with 1906.12170, which adopts Chung's curriculum); the pids are in row 11. Fix the audit too: n=6 with 5 pids, 1806.06053 missing.
6. M03 §3.4 contradiction. All three cited works ([1], [7], [27]) state single-word-first training, not two. Cite [10] Chung 2017 §3.1 for it.
7. Validation split (Outrun bullet 3). State it as an inconsistency: §3.3 says "validation set" while Algorithm 1 line 11 says "training" pairs.
8. Missed internal inconsistencies in the target. Add them under "Where the claims outrun the evidence":
   - Table 1 gives the audio teacher 15.7% WER; Table 4 gives the "Audio Teacher" 17.2% for the same structure.
   - Table 6 is LRW-only, yet §4.2 says Ours-TC is pretrained with the LRS2/LRS3 pretrain sets, and both report 18.7.
   - The B.-2 baseline (24.3) sits in Table 6's "w/ CL" row, which leaves unclear what the 5.6-point gain isolates.
9. SyncVSR condition. Add that its audio tokens come from vq-wav2vec, which was pretrained on external audio, and that it uses a Transformer backend. Note that the 5.0-point gap is not like-for-like.
10. Ref [11] DOI. Correct it to 10.1109/ICCV.2019.00080 and add pp. 713-722. Add DOI 10.1109/ICASSP40776.2020.9054253 to ref [6].

**Low**
11. Verdict confound sentence. Mark the "about half" ratio (4.1 of 7.9 points) as "(inference)".
12. Outrun bullet 2. "A video teacher helps more than an audio teacher" is the authors' own motivating result (§1, Table 1; §3.3). Make the review's own point lead: the video master adds only 0.4 points. Otherwise the bullet does not show a claim that outruns the evidence.
13. M02 row 8. "Fixed-teacher" for Li et al. [9] is not in the Afouras quote. Drop it or label it as unverified.
14. Appendix M02 breakdown. Make it sum to 4 ("fixed teacher 3: ASR 2, video 1; jointly trained video branches 1").
15. The Appendix calls the population "AVSR decoders", but the inclusion regex requires a lip-reading, VSR or LRS/LRW mention. AVSR papers without one (e.g. [9]) fall outside, so say so.
16. Dating, minor. cvf-xu20-aemsr (CVPR 2020) is left "unresolved" but is prior. Apart from this, the windows (prior before 2020-12; concurrent 2020-12 to 2021-12; first public 2021-06, matching Crossref) are applied correctly. No later or concurrent work is treated as prior, and every [concurrent] and [later] label in the text is right.

Clean on the remaining checks: the text is English only, names no model, and carries no process notes or disclaimers. The authors' caveats on CL, MV-LRS and master weakness are credited with section locations.

## Scores

- Procedure: 6/10. Comparator numbers and quotes were checked carefully, and the TM-Seq2Seq, TM-CTC-KD, V2P, born-again and Martinez values all match their sources. The shared population, however, is not a decoder population, and duplicates are not merged. The M03 tally misreads 2 to 3 of 9 snippets. The general-ML precedent for the deciding M02 mechanism was not checked.
- Final text: 6/10. Most numbers are right and the positions are mostly supported. The Verdict's M12 sentence is mis-framed, the LRS3 state-of-the-art line is wrong as stated, and "the design is new" is unscoped. One DOI is wrong.
