Further points checked:

- I found no "ahead" or "behind" verdict and no "first / only" wording. The novelty-like sentence "The paper adds the ResNet …" rests on "[concurrent] 0 of 4 name a ResNet". It should cite the Appendix in the same sentence. I know of no ResNet lipreader on LRW or GRID before 2017-03 (unverified beyond the ledger).
- M13 has the usual approach "Not compared" with the verdict `standard`. Under the rule, "not reported" or a one-line reason fits better.

## Required fixes, ordered by impact

1. **Verdict, reversed reasoning (high).** The pretraining point does not make the 6.8-point margin an overreach, because extra pretraining favours WAS. Either drop "main overreach" or state the actual weakness: a single-run, cross-system comparison, against a figure the proceedings misprint as 74.2% in §5.1 (corrected in arXiv v4).
2. **SyncVSR condition (high).** In the Verdict, the M06 state of the art and the M12 state of the art, replace "LRW video only" with "LRW only, trained with audio-token supervision from LRW audio (vq-wav2vec tokens)". Say that the 10.2-point gap compares an audio-supervised model with an audio-free one. If an audio-free figure is wanted, give [5] at 92.8% with WB.
3. **Counting basis (medium-high).** Pick one unit. Either use keys ("156 keys, 3 of them the target") or deduplicate to 154 studies, with 104 for 3D conv + ResNet and 31 for the per-step loss (inference). State "upper bound, related-work mentions included" in the Verdict and M06 text wherever 106 or 115 carries "the field's default". Give the dated split for the recurrent back end (prior / [concurrent] / [later] counts) so 115 has denominators.
4. **Component table fairness (medium).** The M06 row shows the target's own design as the "usual approach" with `standard`, which reads as if the paper followed the field. Add "today; originated here; 0 of 4 [concurrent] at the time" to the cell or the note. Move M12's "ResNet-18 front end" count out of M12 and count an evaluation protocol there instead.
5. **Recurrent back end at the time (medium).** Add that WAS [3] [concurrent] already used a VGG-M + LSTM encoder on LRW. The target's difference is then the 3D layer, the ResNet trunk, and a classification Bi-LSTM without sentence pretraining.
6. **Labelling (medium-low).** "The parts predate the combination" cites LipNet [concurrent]. Rephrase to "earlier or concurrent work had the parts" or similar.
7. **"One run" stated as fact (medium-low).** The paper gives no run count. Write "no run count or dispersion reported (§4.5)" in the Verdict, M06 and M12 text and in the table cell "LRW top-1, one run".
8. **Per-step loss count (medium-low).** Say that the 33 include temporal averaging of outputs (a different design), or report the pattern without the averaging branch. Add "upper bound" to the M03 Appendix entry.
9. **74.2/76.2 question (low).** Replace the question to the authors with a statement: §5.1 of the proceedings misprints 74.2%, and v4 corrects it to 76.2%, which matches WAS Table 8 (100 − 23.8).
10. **Reference [5] (low).** Change it to ICASSP 2022, pp. 8472–8476, doi:10.1109/ICASSP43922.2022.9746706, with arXiv:2209.01383 as an eprint. Update both the `.bib` entry and `refs.json`.
11. **Table 5 (low).** Credit the authors' own §3 and §5.3 discussion of singular/plural and tense pairs, and say "half of the ten most frequent errors are inflection pairs" rather than "the top confusions are".
12. **Small locators (low).** Use "Tables 3–4" for 79.6→81.5. Mention addition versus concatenation as a second condition on N4 and N5. Optionally note the Table 3 Top-10 anomaly (N5 96.3% < N4 97.4%).

## Scores

- Procedure: 7/10. The sources were read and all but two quotes match exactly. Dating, DOIs (except [5]) and the 74.2/76.2 check are sound. The deductions are for the mixed counting basis, upper-bound counts used as design counts, a slot-mismatched M12 tally, and the per-step pattern sweeping in a different design.
- Final text: 6/10. The numbers are accurate, and the "standard" verdicts follow the rules and judge protocol. But the headline "main overreach" points in the wrong direction, the state-of-the-art gap omits SyncVSR's audio supervision, and the component table presents a design this paper originated as the field's usual approach without saying so.

What works: the ablation arithmetic and its frozen-front-end caveat are right; the 2D-vs-3D contrast with [2] is well chosen; and the 74.2/76.2 discrepancy was traced to arXiv v4.
