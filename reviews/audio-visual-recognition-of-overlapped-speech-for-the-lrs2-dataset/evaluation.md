## Required fixes, by impact

**High**
1. Fig. 2 depth confound (Verdict; M06 gated section; "Where the claims outrun"; "What the field needs next"). Replace "adds 6+6+3 layers, which concatenation lacks" and "deeper gated networks / unmatched depth". Inference from the Fig. 2 labels: RecogNet has 15 / 12 / 9 TDNN-F layers for concat / V⊗A / AV⊗A, so the audio-path depth is 15 / 18 / 15 and the total is 15 / 24 / 24. The best system (AV⊗A) matches concatenation in depth but carries 9 more layers (VisualNet plus FusionNet). Call the confound unmatched parameter count, keep "(inference)", and change the recommendation from "match depth" to "match parameter count". Add a claim-table row that quotes the Fig. 2 RecogNet labels.
2. M12 "behind" (M12 section, Verdict, table). Say explicitly how the rule is met: three earlier mixture AVSR studies were read and each states the interferer source and test size. Name the best one, Barker & Shao 2007, whose public PASCAL SSC lists give 600 pairs per TMR in three speaker-relation conditions. Limit the verdict to test-recipe reporting, since Chao 2016 tests one SNR against the target's four. Reword the Verdict to "the test-interferer source and mixture count are unstated" instead of "the test-mixture recipe is unstated": the reference source, truncation and SNRs are stated. Either say in the text that M11 "not reported" and M12 "behind" rest on the same omission, or let M12 rest on it alone.

**Medium**
3. Drop "repeats that contrast". Shao compares audio-only with audio-visual weight estimation. The target compares video-only with audio-visual gates. Say instead that the target has no audio-driven gate, so Shao's contrast is not repeated.
4. Quote the authors in full: "To the best of our knowledge, the only previous work in this direction was reported in [23]" (§1) and "the only known previous AVSR research for overlapped speech used feature concatenation based fusion [23]" (§1, contribution 2). In the Verdict, note that Chao et al. [3] is cited by the authors themselves, so only Shao & Barker [2] and Barker & Shao [6] are uncited priors.
5. Name the same-group gated design: Liu et al. 2019, "Exploiting visual features using Bayesian gated neural networks for disordered speech recognition" (Interspeech 2019, [concurrent], cited by the target as [26], disordered and not overlapped speech). Change "holds only for neural gates" to "holds for learned feature-level gating on overlapped speech" (Shao's weights also come from an MLP).
6. Lip-reading comparison: add that Afouras's LM is trained on 26M words of subtitles against the target's LM on more than 2M words. The 48.3 vs 48.86 verdict stands, but this states the deciding condition.
7. Yu 2020 gap: add "for multi-channel audio-only systems" to "around 13%".

**Low**
8. "no clean row" → "no clean-test column" (Verdict and the M12 table cell).
9. "one run per system / each system runs once" → "no seeds, variance or significance reported" (the paper does not state runs).
10. SOTA lines: state that Nguyen gives no LM for AV1. Replace "1449 h of AVYT" with "AVYT (1526 h: 1449 h talking-face plus 77 h silent-face)". Mark 1.7% as "as evaluated by Nguyen et al." and consider adding V1 15.7% clean for the lip-reading half of the slot.
11. Claim table: extend the quote t29 to include "significantly outperform". Replace "t70: unstated entry" in row 12 with the Fig. 2 labels.
12. Remove process notes from the reference list: "Read as arXiv v1 ...; DOI and pages from Crossref" (ref 1) and "Table 1 read from the rendered layout" (ref 5). Make "Zhang, S.-X." the same in the header and in reference 1.
13. Question to the authors on speaker disjointness: the authors already say LRS2 has no speaker labels (§4.1). Rephrase it as a question about programme or broadcast-date disjointness.

## What holds (short)
The bibliography and dating are clean. All comparator numbers match their rendered tables (Afouras, Petridis, Nguyen, Shao, Barker, Chao). The central finding on the "only previous work" claim is right: Shao & Barker 2006 drive AV-likelihood-based weights on two-talker mixtures. So is the lip-reading SOTA correction (48.3% with LM against 48.86%). The authors' own caveats (§4.2 sequence training, §5 deferrals) are credited. [Concurrent] labels are right. The text is English only, with no model names and no disclaimers in the body.

## Scores
- Procedure: 7/10. Sources were read, counts are traceable, dating is sound, and the version and DOIs are right. Lost points: the Fig. 2 reading error, the incomplete statement of the "behind" rule, and the missing same-group design.
- Final text: 6/10. One wrong central confound (depth), one wrong analogy (the contrast), a "behind" verdict worded beyond its evidence, and conditions dropped on two quoted numbers.
