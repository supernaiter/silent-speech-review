## Numbered findings (required fixes, ordered by impact)

1. **M06/M12 state of the art cites a baseline row with the wrong year.** "In 2023 the end-to-end WGAN [9] row ... 16.05%" is the 2022 baseline row ("End-to-end WGAN (2022)") inside [3]'s Table VII, not [3]'s own best result. Report [3]'s own GRID unseen-speaker row (method, ESTOI, WER) as the state of the art, or drop the SOTA label. Apply the same fix to the M12 SOTA line and the component table entries for M06 and M12.
2. **"Intelligibility is unsupported" overstates.** The paper reports STOI/ESTOI (objective intelligibility). Rewrite as "no ASR word error is reported" and keep the point that Table 2's baselines have published WER on that split [3].
3. **Contradiction about Facetron's test.** "Where the claims outrun the evidence" says Facetron "has the same input and test type". The M07 section says Facetron tested gender recognition where the target tests identity EER. Change it to "the same input and an unseen-speaker test".
4. **"EER disagrees" conflates two targets.** MOS-FVM measures fit to the face, and EER measures match to the true voice. Reword: the face-match claim rests on 20 sentences and 20 listeners, and EER shows that the true speaker's voice is not recovered (36.72 vs 6.751 for ground truth).
5. **"Chiefly gender (inference, Fig. 2)" lacks support.** Fig. 2 (t-SNE) is quoted as showing speaker-independent content embeddings, not what the face embedding encodes. Either cite evidence about gender (e.g. the gender loss design, Sec. 2.2, framed as design intent) or drop "chiefly gender".
6. **Significance is attached to both ablations, but only the VAE ablation is quoted with p<0.05.** Limit "(paired t-tests, p<0.05)" to Proposed vs Proposed-VAE, or add the quote for Proposed-CML.
7. **The M03 usual approach is not clearly gradient reversal.** By the Appendix tally, gradient reversal is 6 of 18 and content-identity disentanglement 5 of 18, which is the target's own category. State both counts and say that the field has no dominant design.
8. **The V2SFlow SOTA (M07) quote is thin.** The Table I quote stops before the V2SFlow-V row. Add the V2SFlow-V Table I row, or cite Table III for 0.664. Mark "the gap shown here remains" as (inference), because it compares LRS3 SECS with GRID EER.
9. **Component table labels disagree with the section headings** (M07 "Lexicon and LM" vs "Use-time information"; M06 "Processing and decoding" vs "Waveform generation"). Align them.
10. **"Capping all quality scores"**: the Griffin-Lim ground truth (PESQ 3.293 seen, 3.289 unseen) bounds PESQ. The claim does not extend to STOI or MOS without evidence. Narrow it.
11. **"Meets no prior system on any metric"**: reword to "is never compared with a prior system" (Table 1 has no prior systems; Table 2 omits the zero-shot model).
12. **A possible additional deciding prior for M07 (unverified).** Millerdurai, Abdel Khaliq, Ulrich, "Show Me Your Face, And I'll Tell You How You Speak" (arXiv 2206.14009, 2022) reports lip-to-speech with a face-derived speaker embedding for unseen speakers. If confirmed, cite it next to Facetron as prior to the "first attempt" claim. Face-to-voice TTS priors with the same teacher design (face encoder trained toward a speech speaker embedding), such as Face2Speech (Goto et al., Interspeech 2020), could also be named as context for M07 (unverified locators).
13. **Minor procedure issue.** Claim-table row 7 holds a truncated claim ("V-B) [2].") and maps it to the WER quote rather than to the held-out-speaker quote. Fix the row so that each claim carries its own text.
