## Fairness, chronology, format

- Concurrent and later labels are correct. Martinez [7] and Yu [10] are [concurrent]. Whisper-Flamingo, SyncVSR and USR 2.0 are [later]. No concurrent work is called a missed citation. The omitted LRW results (Weng & Kitani 2019-05; Stafylakis CVIU 2018-11) are more than 6 months prior, so it is fair to call them omitted.
- Authors' caveats: the -10 dB caveat is credited. The enhancer lineage (#3) is the one case where an authors' statement is presented as the review's finding.
- English only. No model names, process notes or disclaimers in the review body.

## Scores

- Procedure: 6/10. The table reading, the seven-cell match and the noise-recipe comparison are careful and correct. Against that: the population boundary was applied by title, against the review's own rule; the closer enhancer source cited by the target ([6] there) was not read; the primary like-for-like source for the state of the art was not found; two DOIs were not checked.
- Final text: 5/10. The Verdict mislabels the main gain and misattributes the enhancer. It states two contested points as fact (clean-trained baseline, copied cells) and compares the LRW result against the wrong prior. The component sections are mostly accurate and well hedged with "(inference)".
