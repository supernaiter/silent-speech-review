## Required fixes, by impact

1. **Component table counts (high).** Replace whole-ledger slot-fill counts with design counts and show denominators (e.g. "ECoG, n of 7,388"), or say "slot filled" instead of naming a design. As written, "ECoG (slot-filled 7,329)", "System described (6,075)", "Offline results (82)" and "Paired-audio phone alignment (31)" are false.
2. **M13 verdict (high).** Change `not reported` to `standard`. Describe the real-time system (rtNSR on a Linux i7 machine, bedside runs, latency stated only as "negligible"). Move the missing online accuracy and latency figures to M12 only.
3. **36 dialogue-context matches (medium-high).** Store the regex and the 36 pids with date and a one-line reason each in the M07 audit, and write "inspected as snippets". The stored `fulltext_pattern` gives 8 hits, not 36. My independent pre-2020 scan supports the absence, so the verdict survives, but the stated inspection cannot be verified from the record.
4. **Name the closest prior class (medium).** Add one clause separating the design from P300-speller priors built from previously decoded selections (ledger: `pmc-5495144-lmbci`, 2016 review of Speier et al.). Those priors are linguistic and come from the same user's output, not from a decoded partner utterance.
5. **Fairness on the real-time claim (medium).** Report that the authors state that all results are offline simulations, that they argue the online runs differ only in hyperparameters, and that they claim negligible latency. Then say why this still falls short: no measured online accuracy or latency.
6. **"Closed-set grammar effect" (medium).** Restate it as an untested confound: the set restriction and the soft weighting were not separated, and the hard prior did not improve on the soft prior.
7. **M12 counts (medium).** Drop "(upper bounds)" for the design counts, because the regex misses [2]. Say that none of the 18 online-accuracy hits predates the target. Do not give "online accuracy figure" as the usual approach when it covers 18 of 134.
8. **M06 counting bases (low-medium).** Report deep_network 183 beside hmm_viterbi 142. Explain 360 / 355 / 210 / 7,345, or keep one base per sentence.
9. **Wording (low).** "did not help" becomes "did not improve on the soft prior (§Discussion)". "concede that visible options could affect processing" becomes "note they have no evidence it did and propose testing without visual cues". Unify the M07 slot label.

- Procedure: 6/10. Deciding priors were read, the quotes are exact and the chronology is right. But the dialogue-context inspection is unrecorded and the table counts come from the wrong tally.
- Final text: 6/10. The M07 `ahead` verdict and the numbers hold. The table counts are mislabelled, M13 has the wrong verdict, and the authors' own disclosures are not reported.
