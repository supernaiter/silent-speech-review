# Reviews

## Component reviews

Each paper judged component by component against the usual approach and the state of the art.

- [A First Exploration of Neuromorphic OT-CFM for Multi-Speaker VSR](lipsflow-a-first-exploration-of-neuromorphic-ot-cfm-for-multi-speaker-vsr/) (2026). Event input and a two-step OT-CFM decoder are standard; the DVS-Lip evaluation is behind: WER on an isolated-word set with no split details or seeds, and baselines that do not match their sources.
- [A cross-species neural foundation model for end-to-end speech decoding](a-cross-species-neural-foundation-model-for-end-to-end-speech-decoding/) (2026). Cascaded decoding with the pretrained encoder is a strong standard result; the end-to-end audio-LLM path beats the prior end-to-end entry but trails RNN cascades, and the cross-species gain is small.
- [AVSRBench: A Multi-Condition AVSR Benchmark](avsrbench-a-multi-condition-avsr-benchmark/) (2026). Zero-shot cross-corpus AVSR testing (M12) and a video-call test set (M11) are standard after WildVSR; each condition mixes several shifts, without repeated runs.
- [Affect Decoding in Phonated and Silent Speech Production from Surface EMG](affect-decoding-in-phonated-and-silent-speech-production-from-surface-emg/) (2026). Affect decoded from silent-articulation EMG, a design absent from 99 ledger studies (M08, ahead); it works within subject, nears chance across speakers, and is not separated from facial expression.
- [Brain-to-Speech: Prosody Feature Engineering and Transformer-Based Reconstruction](brain-to-speech-prosody-feature-engineering-and-transformer-based-reconstruction/) (2026). Same three parts and same Table 1 values as MiSTR (2025, same first two authors), not cited. Features (M06) and phase vocoder (M08) standard, unablated; no listener test (M12, behind).
- [Brain2Speech-Net: Fast and Intelligible Brain-to-Speech Synthesis Without Text Decoding](brain2speech-net-intelligible-real-time-brain-to-speech-synthesis-without-text-decoding/) (2026). Text-free synthesis is shown, but its real time is throughput, not causal synthesis, unseen-sentence WER is 2.3 times the cascade, and intelligibility rests on ratings and ASR.
- [Comparison of sEMG Encoding Accuracy Across Speech Modes Using Articulatory and Phoneme Features](comparison-of-semg-encoding-accuracy-across-speech-modes-using-articulatory-and-phoneme-features/) (2026). Applies an ECoG articulatory-versus-phoneme encoding comparison (Chartier 2018) to sEMG in three speech modes; the gain is small, mode contrasts are confounded, and no decoder is tested.
- [Cross-Modal Masking for Robust Silent Speech Synthesis Using sEMG and Lipreading](cross-modal-masking-for-robust-silent-speech-synthesis-using-semg-and-lipreading/) (2026). Speech synthesis from fused sEMG and lip video is new to the ledger (M05, ahead); span masking (M03) adds a non-significant clean gain; seen speakers only (M12, behind).
- [Do EEG Foundation Models Transfer to Speech? A Benchmark on Overt and Imagined Speech Decoding](do-eeg-foundation-models-transfer-to-speech-a-benchmark-on-overt-and-imagined-speech-decoding/) (2026). Benchmark (M03) confirms earlier reports that LaBraM gains nothing on speech EEG; decoders (M06) sit far below same-corpus results; test-subject early stopping (M12).
- [EEG-Based Imagined Speech Decoding Using a Hybrid CNN-SNN Architecture](eeg-based-imagined-speech-decoding-using-a-hybrid-cnn-snn-architecture/) (2026). CNN-to-LIF decoder (M06) is standard: a 2024 SNN-CNN inner-speech decoder precedes it; 80.13% on the validation subset is not comparable with the 82.6% competition test score (M12); no SNN ablation.
- [EMG-to-Speech with Fewer Channels](emg-to-speech-with-fewer-channels/) (2026). Halving EMG electrodes costs synthesis the same ~35% relative WER known from recognition; initialization helps, channel dropout does not, and a weak unrepeated baseline undercuts the ranking.
- [Lip-Siri: Contactless Open-Sentence Silent Speech with Wi-Fi Backscatter](lip-siri-contactless-open-sentence-silent-speech-with-wi-fi-backscatter/) (2026). Wi-Fi backscatter lip sensing (M05) and subword sentence output (M08) are standard; open-sentence decoding is untested, with no sentence-disjoint split and 36.87% WER.
- [LipSody: Lip-to-Speech Synthesis with Enhanced Prosody Consistency](lipsody-lip-to-speech-synthesis-with-enhanced-prosody-consistency/) (2026). Pitch and energy predicted from lips, face and emotion (M06) is standard: Faces-to-Voices did it in 2025. Prosody metrics improve, WER does not; normalization change unablated.
- [Mechanistic Interpretability of Brain-to-Speech Models Across Speech Modes](mechanistic-interpretability-of-brain-to-speech-models-across-speech-modes/) (2026). Patching shows the decoder depends on donor activations, not that transfer runs through a compact subspace: kept regions score below the unpatched decoder, with no null and no held-out split.
- [Multi-Subject Pretraining Enables Short-Calibration Personalization for Closed-Corpus Surface EMG Speech Decoding](multi-subject-pretraining-enables-short-calibration-personalization-for-closed-corpus-surface-emg-speech-decoding/) (2026). Standard training recipe (M03) at larger scale: pretrain on 26, calibrate 3 min. The closed corpus carries the result: unseen sentences give 99.9% WER (M12, behind).
- [NasoVoce: A Nose-Mounted Low-Audibility Speech Interface for Always-Available Speech Interaction](nasovoce/) (2026). Nose-pad mic plus vibration sensor (M05) and dual-input fusion (M06) are standard; noise is added to the mic only, so the robustness claim is untested in real noise (M12, behind).
- [Physiological Noise Augmentation Improves Non-Invasive Brain-to-Speech](physiological-noise-augmentation-improves-non-invasive-brain-to-speech/) (2026). Artifact-remix augmentation repeats 2024 reference-signal mixing, gains 3.3 points (not 4.7) only with 10-trial averaging, barely beats white noise, and does not directly test the claimed invariance.
- [Relating the Neural Representations of Vocalized, Mimed, and Imagined Speech](relating-the-neural-representations-of-vocalized-mimed-and-imagined-speech/) (2026). Cross-mode transfer and rank scoring are standard tests; one participant, unstated alignment to vocalized targets and mismatched nulls leave the hierarchy and linear-better claims unsupported.
- [SLD-L2S: Hierarchical Subspace Latent Diffusion for High-Fidelity Lip to Speech Synthesis](sld-l2s-hierarchical-subspace-latent-diffusion-for-high-fidelity-lip-to-speech-synthesis/) (2026). Codec-latent flow decoder (M06) is standard: quality lead rests on predictors that rate output above ground truth; WER trails V2SFlow; speaker similarity uses reference audio.
- [SilentWear: an Ultra-Low Power Wearable System for EMG-based Silent Speech Recognition](silentwear-an-ultra-low-power-wearable-system-for-emg-based-silent-speech-recognition/) (2026). Neckband EMG with on-body inference at 20.5 mW (M13) but no on-device accuracy; repositioning recovery by labeled fine-tuning is standard (M03); sensor reused from 2025.
- [Soft Active EMG Interface for Machine Learning-Enabled Silent Speech Recognition](soft-active-electromyography-interface-for-machine-learning-enabled-silent-speech-recognition/) (2026). Hand-pressed fingertip EMG (M05) is standard: privacy and noise benefits argued, not tested; 97.2% on 30 words rests on a random within-participant split with no session hold-out (M12, behind).
- [SoniSpeech: A Large-Scale Open-Vocabulary Tri-Modal Dataset for Wearable Silent Speech Interfaces](sonispeech-a-large-scale-open-vocabulary-tri-modal-dataset-for-wearable-silent-speech-interfaces/) (2026). Open-vocabulary acoustic sensing on glasses (M05) and a one-speaker paired corpus (M11) are standard; voiced-data gains follow 2020 EMG work; one-speaker, one-run tests lag the field (M12).
- [Toward Practical BCI: A Real-time Wireless Imagined Speech EEG Decoding System](toward-practical-bci-a-real-time-wireless-imagined-speech-eeg-decoding-system/) (2026). Not ahead on any component: consumer headsets and imagined-speech user identification predate it; no online accuracy, latency or split, so the real-time practical claim is unsupported.
- [Transfer Learning from ImageNet for MEG-Based Decoding of Imagined Speech](transfer-learning-from-imagenet-for-meg-based-decoding-of-imagined-speech/) (2026). ImageNet transfer on MEG scalograms (M03) and the learned sensor projection (M06) are standard; the ablation is new; rest and reading-order confounds limit the binary tasks (M12).
- [Zero-Shot Imagined Speech Decoding via Imagined-to-Listened MEG Mapping](zero-shot-imagined-speech-decoding-via-imagined-to-listened-meg-mapping/) (2026). New pipeline (imagined MEG mapped to listened MEG, then a listened-trained word decoder), but no no-mapping or null-mapping word control, and a shared closed poem set confounds word decoding.
- [iPhoneme: Brain-to-Text Communication for ALS Using ConformerXL Decoding](iphoneme-brain-to-text-communication-for-als-using-conformerxl-decoding/) (2026). Behind: 26.61% WER is four times the 6.67% holdout baseline on the same recordings; the T15 best-result claim fails, the scoring split is unnamed and the gaze interface is untested.
- [A Convolutional Framework for Mapping Imagined Auditory MEG into Listened Brain Responses](a-convolutional-framework-for-mapping-imagined-auditory-meg-into-listened-brain-responses/) (2025). Mapping imagined onto listened MEG (M06) is ahead in design, but the subject-calibrated CNN gain is unproven: no ablation, a channel-shuffling null, and r 0.0113 against 0.0063.
- [A Silent Speech Decoding System from EEG and EMG with Heterogenous Electrode Configurations](a-silent-speech-decoding-system-from-eeg-and-emg-with-heterogenous-electrode-configurations/) (2025). Pooled EEG/EMG pretraining lifts 64-word accuracy, but each test subject's training sessions, the patient's included, are in pretraining; the coordinate kernel is neither new nor best.
- [An instantaneous voice-synthesis neuroprosthesis](an-instantaneous-voice-synthesis-neuroprosthesis/) (2025). Closed-loop sentence voice from intracortical arrays holds for one participant; the design is standard, forced-choice and proxy-target metrics overstate intelligibility.
- [Articulatory Feature Prediction from Surface EMG during Speech Production](articulatory-feature-prediction-from-surface-emg-during-speech-production/) (2025). Articulatory route works on vocalized EMG but trails the mel baseline on WER; it exists for ECoG since 2019, and the electrode subset is never tested against equal-size alternatives.
- [Brain-to-Text Decoding: A Non-invasive Approach via Typing](brain-to-text-decoding-a-non-invasive-approach-via-typing/) (2025). Executed typing as the decoded action (M04) was ahead for MEG/EEG in 2025; keystroke-locked decoding (M06) and an n-gram LM (M07) are standard, and decoding needs known keystroke times.
- [CAT-Net: A Cross-Attention Tone Network for Cross-Subject EEG-EMG Fusion Tone Decoding](cat-net-a-cross-attention-tone-network-for-cross-subject-eeg-emg-fusion-tone-decoding/) (2025). Cross-attention EEG-EMG fusion is new among EEG-EMG decoders but rests on a five-fold ablation with no seed count; no EEG-only result, and the subject adversary adds 0.41 points.
- [Confidence-Based Self-Training for EMG-to-Speech: Leveraging Synthetic EMG for Robust Modeling](confidence-based-self-training-for-emg-to-speech-leveraging-synthetic-emg-for-robust-modeling/) (2025). Synthetic EMG with confidence filtering (M03, standard) gives a small real-EMG gain, 23.30% to 21.87% WER on one speaker; large gains appear only on synthetic test EMG.
- [Conformer-based Ultrasound-to-Speech Conversion](conformer-based-ultrasound-to-speech-conversion/) (2025). Behind on M06: the Conformer reads beam lines of one frame, missing the multi-frame context mappers have used since 2020, with no objective gain; a small naturalness gain, no intelligibility test.
- [EEG-to-Voice Decoding of Spoken and Imagined speech Using Non-Invasive EEG](eeg-to-voice-decoding-of-spoken-and-imagined-speech-using-non-invasive-eeg/) (2025). Drops DTW from an EEG-to-Mel generator (M06, standard) but tests only the ten trained classes (M12, behind); one frozen ASR both trains and scores it, and EEG output beats recorded-speech WER.
- [From Silent Signals to Natural Language: A Dual-Stage Transformer-LLM Approach](from-silent-signals-to-natural-language-a-dual-stage-transformer-llm-approach/) (2025). GPT-2 correction of one ASR transcript is neither first nor near the field: LLM correction of silent-EMG hypotheses already reached 12.2% WER on this benchmark; here 30% on ~100 utterances, one run.
- [LTA-L2S: Lexical Tone-Aware Lip-to-Speech Synthesis for Mandarin with Cross-Lingual Transfer Learning](lta-l2s-lexical-tone-aware-lip-to-speech-synthesis-for-mandarin-with-cross-lingual-transfer-lear/) (2025). Mandarin lip-to-speech whose gain over Intelligible matches its English-initialization ablation; the tone components move CER little with no repeats reported, and pitch removal leaves TER at 40.9%.
- [Landmark Guided Visual Feature Extractor for Visual Speech Recognition with Limited Resource](landmark-guided-visual-feature-extractor-for-visual-speech-recognition-with-limited-resource/) (2025). Lip-landmark graph plus pixel fusion is a standard design (2020 precedent); the 0.90-point low-data gain comes from one unseeded run, and LRW 91.69% stays below 95.0%.
- [Lightweight Diffusion-based Framework for Online Imagined Speech Decoding in Aphasia](lightweight-diffusion-based-framework-for-online-imagined-speech-decoding-in-aphasia/) (2025). Diffusion EEG decoder (M06) standard: Diff-E lineage, no ablation, not smaller. Online use (M13) standard: one patient, 20 trials. M12: Table I overall row disagrees with class rows.
- [LipDiffuser: Lip-to-Speech Generation with Conditional Diffusion Models](lipdiffuser-lip-to-speech-generation-with-conditional-diffusion-models/) (2025). Diffusion decoder with per-frame MP-FiLM (M06) and audio-only pretraining (M02) are standard; quality and speaker-similarity leads rest on enhanced targets and a clean-reference enrollment.
- [MultiDiffNet: A Multi-Objective Diffusion Framework for Generalizable Brain Decoding](multidiffnet-a-multi-objective-diffusion-framework-for-generalizable-brain-decoding/) (2025). Diff-E-style decoder (M06) whose own ablation does not show diffusion helping; 1.01-point imagined-speech gain over EEGNet on two held-out subjects, normalized on scored trials (M03).
- [NaturalL2S: End-to-End High-quality Multispeaker Lip-to-Speech Synthesis with Differential Digital Signal Processing](naturall2s-end-to-end-high-quality-multispeaker-lip-to-speech-synthesis-with-differential-digita/) (2025). DDSP prior and jointly trained vocoder (M06) are standard: joint waveform training predates it; quality leads its baselines, but LRS3 WER 30.4% trails the uncompared LipVoicer (21.4%).
- [NeuroTTT: Bridging Pretrain–Downstream Task Misalignment in EEG Foundation Models via Test-Time Training](neurottt-bridging-pretraining-downstream-task-misalignment-in-eeg-foundation-models-via-test-tim/) (2025). SSL fine-tuning losses (M02) and test-time updates (M03) are standard: SSL-TTT never beats Tent; 0.59 balanced accuracy trails the 82.6% competition winner if scored on its test set.
- [Non-invasive electromyographic speech neuroprosthesis: a geometric perspective](non-invasive-electromyographic-speech-neuroprosthesis-a-geometric-perspective/) (2025). Silent-EMG-to-text without vocalized pairing and SPD inputs are both standard: subvocal-only label training predates it, and 73.53% WER from one person is tested only against a near-chance baseline.
- [Poster: Recognizing Hidden-in-the-Ear Private Key for Reliable Silent Speech Interface Using Multi-Task Learning](poster-recognizing-hidden-in-the-ear-private-key-for-reliable-silent-speech-interface-using-mult/) (2025). Sensing, shared model and joint word-plus-identity output are each standard; whisper adds nothing over the 91.4% ultrasonic-only baseline, and impostors are training participants (M12 behind).
- [Pretraining Large Brain Language Model for Active BCI: Silent Speech](pretraining-large-brain-language-model-for-active-bci-silent-speech/) (2025). In-domain EEG pretraining adds 4.3 points on 24 mouthed words (35.3% to 39.6%), one session, no seeds; Du-IN showed this earlier. Muscle artefact and new users remain untested.
- [RESOUND: Speech Reconstruction from Silent Videos via Acoustic-Semantic Decomposed Modeling](resound-speech-reconstruction-from-silent-videos-via-acoustic-semantic-decomposed-modeling/) (2025). Decoder split (M06) and lip-reader text plus audio prompt (M07) are standard; WER gain comes from the lip reader, LRS3 WER level with LipVoicer, prosody leads confounded by the prompt.
- [Reconstructing Unseen Sentences from Speech-related Biosignals for Open-vocabulary Neural Communication](reconstructing-unseen-sentences-from-speech-related-biosignals-for-open-vocabulary-neural-commun/) (2025). EEG+EMG fusion (M05) and overt-audio DTW targets (M02) are standard; the unseen-sentence claim lacks a cohort WER (M12), and no EMG-only arm or EMG regression is reported.
- [Silent Speech Sentence Recognition with Six-Axis Accelerometers using Conformer and CTC Algorithm](silent-speech-sentence-recognition-with-six-axis-accelerometers-using-conformer-and-ctc-algorith/) (2025). CTC decoder (M06), concatenated training sentences (M03) and six IMUs (M05) are standard: CTC silent-phrase decoding predates it; 97.17% is a word ratio on 4 people, split unstated.
- [Speaking Without Sound: Multi-speaker Silent Speech Voicing with Facial Inputs Only](speaking-without-sound-multi-speaker-silent-speech-voicing-with-facial-inputs-only/) (2025). Face voices (M08) extend 2020 TTS and 2023 lip-to-speech designs to EMG; the pitch-flattening gain (M02) is within seed noise; silent WER 38.91% has no external baseline.
- [Subject-Independent Imagined Speech Detection via Cross-Subject Generalization and Calibration](subject-independent-imagined-speech-detection-via-cross-subject-generalization-and-calibration/) (2025). Cyclic training and 10% target calibration (M03) are standard; uncalibrated detection is near chance (MCC 0.044–0.123), and labels are timed from overt speech.
- [Towards Unified Neural Decoding with Brain Functional Network Modeling](towards-unified-neural-decoding-with-brain-functional-network-modeling/) (2025). Pooled sEEG training (M03) and region-token input with imputation (M06) are standard: both predate it (Du-IN, Chen 2024); no absolute accuracy is given and the baselines are weak checkpoints.
- [Transfer Learning for Covert Speech Classification Using EEG Hilbert Envelope and Temporal Fine Structure](transfer-learning-for-covert-speech-classification-using-eeg-hilbert-envelope-and-temporal-fine-/) (2025). Overt-to-covert EEG transfer (M04) is not new, even on this dataset; the overt-only control is missing, the from-scratch baseline is unmatched, and 79.82% is tied to no fine-tuning fraction.
- [VSpeechLM: A Visual Speech Language Model for Visual Text-to-Speech Task](vspeechlm-a-visual-speech-language-model-for-visual-text-to-speech-task/) (2025). Lip-timed phonemes into a codec LM (M06) are standard; Chem WER edges EmoDubber 12.5% vs 12.8%, no SyncNet scores, and the stated sync gains do not follow from Table 1.
- [Wireless Silent Speech Interface Using Multi-Channel Textile EMG Sensors Integrated into Headphones](wireless-silent-speech-interface-using-multi-channel-textile-emg-sensors-integrated-into-headpho/) (2025). Headphone-integrated textile EMG (M05) is standard, after a dry-electrode necklace; 96% on ten words is one random sample split over four people, speaker separation not stated.
- [emg2speech: Synthesizing speech from electromyography using self-supervised speech models](emg2speech-synthesizing-speech-from-electromyography-using-self-supervised-speech-models/) (2025). Ports a 2023 ECoG recipe (text-to-speech HuBERT units, CTC) to EMG; phoneme guidance (M02) and covariance input (M06) are standard. Table 6 mixes listener and decoder scoring, so it ranks no route.
- [AKVSR: Audio Knowledge Empowered Visual Speech Recognition by Compressing Audio Knowledge of a Pretrained Model](akvsr-audio-knowledge-empowered-visual-speech-recognition-by-compressing-audio-knowledge-of-a-pretrained-model/) (2024). The quantized audio memory (M02) is a standard memory-recall design; its gains repeat over a no-memory baseline, but the LRS3 state-of-the-art claim and the edge over distillation do not hold.
- [An Accurate and Rapidly Calibrating Speech Neuroprosthesis](an-accurate-and-rapidly-calibrating-speech-neuroprosthesis/) (2024). Reaches 2.5% online large-vocabulary WER and 248 h of home use in one participant; rapid calibration is a cross-study claim and the 97.5% conversation accuracy matches the Copy Task figure.
- [Let There Be Sound: Reconstructing High Quality Speech from Silent Videos](let-there-be-sound-reconstructing-high-quality-speech-from-silent-videos/) (2024). HuBERT-unit, pitch and energy targets (M02) and flow post-net (M06) are standard, after ReVISE and VCVTS; GRID WER 17.07% vs 17.62%; seen-speaker tests only (M12 behind).
- [LipVoicer: Generating Speech from Silent Videos Guided by Lip Reading](lipvoicer-generating-speech-from-silent-videos-guided-by-lip-reading/) (2024). Lip-reader text conditioning (M07) is new but standard: words come from the lip reader (19.1% reading WER becomes 21.4%; 86.2% unguided), and no cascade control is run.
- [Ultrasensitive Textile Strain Sensors Redefine Wearable Silent Speech Interfaces with High Machine Learning Efficiency](ultrasensitive-textile-strain-sensors-redefine-wearable-silent-speech-interfaces-with-high-machine-learning-efficiency/) (2024). Standard sensor (M05) and decoder (M06): one throat strain channel, small CNN. 95.25% on 20 words, with split grouping and production mode unstated, does not show silent, session-independent decoding.
- [A high-performance neuroprosthesis for speech decoding and avatar control](a-high-performance-neuroprosthesis-for-speech-decoding-and-avatar-control/) (2023). Text, voice and avatar from ECoG in one participant hold as a demonstration; sensor and decoder are standard, and an LM trained on the test pool inflates the text result.
- [A high-performance speech neuroprosthesis](a-high-performance-speech-neuroprosthesis/) (2023). Large-vocabulary attempted-speech decoding in one participant holds; sensor, decoder and LM were already in the field, and the resolution attribution and cross-study ratios outrun the evidence.
- [Adaptation of Tongue Ultrasound-Based Silent Speech Interfaces Using Spatial Transformer Networks](adaptation-of-tongue-ultrasound-based-silent-speech-interfaces-using-spatial-transformer-networks/) (2023). STN-only adaptation (M03) closes 75-76% of the retraining gap, not the 88% the abstract states; untested against lower-layer adaptation; dev-set MSE only (M12 behind).
- [Audio-visual video-to-speech synthesis with synthesized input audio](audio-visual-video-to-speech-synthesis-with-synthesized-input-audio/) (2023). A second synthesis pass on first-pass speech (M06) and modality dropout (M03) are standard; gains are small and inconsistent, earlier systems keep lower WER, and no equal-training control exists.
- [Decoding speech perception from non-invasive brain recordings](decoding-speech-perception-from-non-invasive-brain-recordings/) (2023). Contrastive M/EEG decoding (M06) and subject-layer pooling (M03) were ahead in 2022 and are now standard; tests reuse training participants, and EEG headline and EEG zero-shot claims outrun the data.
- [IR-UWB Radar-Based Contactless Silent Speech Recognition of Vowels, Consonants, Words, and Phrases](ir-uwb-radar-based-contactless-silent-speech-recognition-of-vowels-consonants-words-and-phrases/) (2023). FERASEC is a standard engineered radar front end with a large gain over 2016 features; phoneme novelty holds only for consonant syllables, and evaluation is within-speaker.
- [Improving the Gap in Visual Speech Recognition Between Normal and Silent Speech Based on Metric Learning](improving-the-gap-in-visual-speech-recognition-between-normal-and-silent-speech-based-on-metric-learning/) (2023). Joint normal-silent training does most of the gap closing; the viseme KL terms add 0.93 WER points with no run count reported, so the metric-learning gain is not established.
- [Intelligible Lip-to-Speech Synthesis with Speech Units](intelligible-lip-to-speech-synthesis-with-speech-units/) (2023). Unit target (M02) and unit-conditioned vocoder (M06) are standard; units cut LRS3 WER 21.8 points, AV-HuBERT 20.4 more, the vocoder 0.3; unit gain untested on AV-HuBERT, single runs.
- [Knowledge Distilled Ensemble Model for sEMG-based Silent Speech Interface](knowledge-distilled-ensemble-model-for-semg-based-silent-speech-interface/) (2023). Ensemble-to-student distillation (M02, M06, M13) is standard: same-modality distillation predates it; 85.9% is one unseeded split of unstated unit, and 7x smaller is against six models.
- [Large-scale unsupervised audio pre-training for video-to-speech synthesis](large-scale-unsupervised-audio-pre-training-for-video-to-speech-synthesis/) (2023). Audio-only decoder pre-training (M03) is standard after TTS decoder transfer; it lowers WER in 7 of 16 pairs, earlier systems keep lower WER, and no equal-training control or listener test exists.
- [LipLearner: Customizable Silent Speech Interactions on Mobile Devices](liplearner-customizable-silent-speech-interactions-on-mobile-devices/) (2023). All three changed components are standard: on-device few-shot head untested against prototypes or fine-tuning; voiced enrollment loses 5.6 points; visual KWS predates it uncited.
- [RobustL2S: Speaker-Specific Lip-to-Speech Synthesis exploiting Self-Supervised Representations](robustl2s-speaker-specific-lip-to-speech-synthesis-exploiting-self-supervised-representations/) (2023). SSL-feature target (M02) and Seq2Seq-to-unit-vocoder decoder (M06) are standard after ReVISE; GRID-4S WER 11.21% is set against baselines scored with other ASRs.
- [Speech Reconstruction from Silent Tongue and Lip Articulation By Pseudo Target Generation and Domain Adversarial Training](speech-reconstruction-from-silent-tongue-and-lip-articulation-by-pseudo-target-generation-and-domain-adversarial-training/) (2023). DTW pseudo targets (M02) follow prior EMG target transfer; the mode discriminator (M03) is untested by its ablation; silent MCD/STOI use self-made references and 18 speakers are dropped.
- [WESPER: Zero-shot and Realtime Whisper to Normal Voice Conversion for Whisper-based Speech Interactions](wesper-zero-shot-and-realtime-whisper-to-normal-voice-conversion-for-whisper-based-speech-interactions/) (2023). WESPER trains a whisper-to-normal converter with no paired data (M02, ahead); its unit route (M06) is untested against baselines, and its ASR gain trails a recognizer fine-tuned on labelled whisper.
- [Zero-Shot Personalized Lip-to-Speech Synthesis with Face Image Based Voice Control](zero-shot-personalized-lip-to-speech-synthesis-with-face-image-based-voice-control/) (2023). Face-image voice control (M07) and VAE/cross-modal training (M03) are standard: Facetron did face-derived zero-shot voice on GRID in 2021; face EER 36.72 vs 26.57 with reference audio; no WER.
- [FastLTS: Non-Autoregressive End-to-End Unconstrained Lip-to-Speech Synthesis](fastlts-non-autoregressive-end-to-end-unconstrained-lip-to-speech-synthesis/) (2022). Parallel decoder, learned-feature GAN generator and transformer front end (M06) are standard; 9.14x mel speedup is real, but evaluation (M12) drops the STOI/ESTOI its Lip2Wav baseline reported.
- [Improved Processing of Ultrasound Tongue Videos by Combining ConvLSTM and 3D Convolutional Networks](improved-processing-of-ultrasound-tongue-videos-by-combining-convlstm-and-3d-convolutional-networks/) (2022). ConvLSTM top layer (M06) is standard: new to ultrasound-to-speech, but a 2.1% MSE edge over BiLSTM, one speaker, one run; later work dropped it. No MCD or listener test (M12 behind).
- [Learning Audio-Visual Speech Representation by Masked Multimodal Cluster Prediction](learning-audio-visual-speech-representation-by-masked-multimodal-cluster-prediction/) (2022). AV-HuBERT's masked audio-visual cluster pre-training (M03) was a large step past distillation, but its LRS3 gains lack repeated runs and later work beats it at matched budgets.
- [Lip-to-Speech Synthesis for Arbitrary Speakers in the Wild](lip-to-speech-synthesis-for-arbitrary-speakers-in-the-wild/) (2022). VAE-GAN lip-to-speech with a reference-audio voice: a standard design whose gains are in distribution and sync metrics; no WER is reported, behind its own lineage on evaluation.
- [Movement Detection of Tongue and Related Body Parts Using IR-UWB Radar](movement-detection-of-tongue-and-related-body-parts-using-ir-uwb-radar/) (2022). Chin-aimed radar and an envelope + GMM-HMM front end are standard; the binary rest-vs-touch task shows some closed-mouth movement, not tongue-specific sensing.
- [SVTS: Scalable Video-to-Speech Synthesis](svts-scalable-video-to-speech-synthesis/) (2022). Conformer mel predictor plus pretrained vocoder (M06) and data scaling (M03) are standard; GRID/LRW gains hold, but later tests put SVTS at 78-82% WER on LRS3.
- [Sequence-to-Sequence Voice Reconstruction for Silent Speech in a Tonal Language](sequence-to-sequence-voice-reconstruction-for-silent-speech-in-a-tonal-language/) (2022). Duration-regulated seq2seq with toneme loss voices silent Mandarin EMG; gains rest on the auxiliary loss, not the architecture, and prompt grouping in the utterance split is unreported.
- [Sub-word Level Lip Reading With Visual Attention](sub-word-level-lip-reading-with-visual-attention/) (2022). Attention pooling of the lip map (M06) was ahead in 2021 but not the route to later SOTA; WordPiece output (M08) was preceded by BPE in 2020; the SOTA table mixes data budgets.
- [Towards Neural Decoding of Imagined Speech based on Spoken Speech](towards-neural-decoding-of-imagined-speech-based-on-spoken-speech/) (2022). Spoken-to-imagined EEG transfer (M04) is the standard overt-to-covert design; 26.8% vs 30.5% on 5 classes, no chance level, and "comparable" rests on a non-significant omnibus test.
- [VCVTS: Multi-Speaker Video-to-Speech Synthesis via Cross-Modal Knowledge Transfer from Voice Conversion](vcvts-multi-speaker-video-to-speech-synthesis-via-cross-modal-knowledge-transfer-from-voice-conversion/) (2022). VCVTS's unit targets, speech-trained decoder and F0 predictor (M02, M03, M06) came early but are untested by ablation: standard. No WER, unlike its baselines: evaluation (M12) behind.
- [Visual Speech Recognition for Multiple Languages in the Wild](visual-speech-recognition-for-multiple-languages-in-the-wild/) (2022). Joint distillation losses are a standard idea worth 1.5-2.0 LRS3 points, less than recipe and masking; LRS3 31.5% trails 16.9% from public data; 4 of 6 languages lack external baselines.
- [An Improved Model for Voicing Silent Speech](an-improved-model-for-voicing-silent-speech/) (2021). Conv-plus-Transformer EMG encoder (M06) is reused by the 2026 state of the art; the phoneme loss (M02) follows 2018 ultrasound work. Single runs; ablations explain 16.3 of 25.8 points.
- [Convolutional Neural Network-Based Age Estimation Using B-Mode Ultrasound Tongue Image](convolutional-neural-network-based-age-estimation-using-b-mode-ultrasound-tongue-image/) (2021). Age output from tongue ultrasound (M08) is new but unproven: the random frame split shares children between training and validation (M12), so speaker identity can explain the gain.
- [EMA2S: An End-to-End Multimodal Articulatory-to-Speech System](ema2s-an-end-to-end-multimodal-articulatory-to-speech-system/) (2021). Speech-latent target (M02) has a 2017 precedent and small, mixed single-run gains; spectrogram-loss-only end-to-end loses to WORLD (M06); a preference test replaces listener transcription (M12).
- [End-to-End Silent Speech Recognition with Acoustic Sensing](end-to-end-silent-speech-recognition-with-acoustic-sensing/) (2021). Standard on sensing and decoding: phone-sonar phase features and an attention decoder work on 54 mouthed sentences of 29 words; the cross-user claim rests on tuning data that include the test users.
- [Improving Neural Silent Speech Interface Models by Adversarial Training](improving-neural-silent-speech-interface-models-by-adversarial-training/) (2021). Standard on the adversarial loss (M02): it predates the paper in video-to-speech and its gains are small, with no variance reported; behind on evaluation (M12): no listening test.
- [Multi-modality Associative Bridging through Memory: Speech Sound Recollected from Face Video](multi-modality-associative-bridging-through-memory-speech-sound-recollected-from-face-video/) (2021). Audio memory recall (M02) is ahead as a design, but the LRW state-of-the-art claim misses a prior 87.9% model, the edge over distillation is one run, and GRID gains are small.
- [Neural Speaker Embeddings for Ultrasound-based Silent Speech Interfaces](neural-speaker-embeddings-for-ultrasound-based-silent-speech-interfaces/) (2021). Standard on embedding conditioning (2.4% relative MSE gain on seen speakers); behind on speaker recognition, which never separates speaker from recording session.
- [Neuroprosthesis for decoding speech in a paralyzed person with anarthria](neuroprosthesis-for-decoding-speech-in-a-paralyzed-person-with-anarthria/) (2021). Sentence decoding from a person who cannot speak holds for 50 words and one participant; the decoder is standard for 2021, and an LM restricted to the test vocabulary drives the gain.
- [Silent Speech and Emotion Recognition from Vocal Tract Shape Dynamics in Real-Time MRI](silent-speech-and-emotion-recognition-from-vocal-tract-shape-dynamics-in-real-time-mri/) (2021). LipNet decoder on rtMRI (M06) and new sentence-text output (M08) are standard; one unseen-speaker split without repeats; a shared 460-sentence list likely puts training sentences in the test.
- [Silent versus modal multi-speaker speech recognition from ultrasound and video](silent-versus-modal-multi-speaker-speech-recognition-from-ultrasound-and-video/) (2021). Standard on both changed components: modal-to-silent loss and fMLLR were known; later work narrows the absolute gap but not the fMLLR ratio, and adaptation gains rest on single runs.
- [Speaker disentanglement in video-to-speech conversion](speaker-disentanglement-in-video-to-speech-conversion/) (2021). Speaker-conditioned video-to-speech with adversarial identity removal: both standard (gradient reversal on GRID lips dates from 2017); EER uses the training embedding network; one run.
- [Speech Synthesis from Text and Ultrasound Tongue Image-based Articulatory Input](speech-synthesis-from-text-and-ultrasound-tongue-image-based-articulatory-input/) (2021). Text+ultrasound input to DNN-TTS (M06) is standard: test MCD falls 0.015-0.229 dB on 8 speakers; BAP, voicing worsen on test, F0 correlation improves; no listening test.
- [3D Convolutional Neural Networks for Ultrasound-Based Silent Speech Interfaces](3d-convolutional-neural-networks-for-ultrasound-based-silent-speech-interfaces/) (2020). (2+1)D front end (M06) is standard: established in lip reading, ported to ultrasound; a 6.3% MSE edge over CNN+LSTM, one speaker, one run; MSE-only evaluation (M12 behind).
- [Brain2Char: a deep architecture for decoding text from brain recordings](brain2char-a-deep-architecture-for-decoding-text-from-brain-recordings/) (2020). Character CTC decoding from ECoG; all changes are standard. The 7.0-10.6% WER relies on repeated sentences, as the authors concede, and unseen sentences average 80% WER.
- [Continuous Silent Speech Recognition using EEG](continuous-silent-speech-recognition-using-eeg/) (2020). Standard on silent reading (M04) and CTC decoding (M06); 83% WER on 30 sentences repeated in training, with an undisclosed 4-gram LM and no chance baseline, does not show EEG decoding.
- [Digital Voicing of Silent Speech](digital-voicing-of-silent-speech/) (2020). Silent-EMG target transfer (M02) extends a 2016 DTW recipe; the open corpus scored by WER (M12) became the shared test bed. Headline 94% gain is closed-vocabulary, 20 clips, one evaluator.
- [Ultra2Speech - A Deep Learning Framework for Formant Frequency Estimation and Tracking from Ultrasound Tongue Images](ultra2speech-a-deep-learning-framework-for-formant-frequency-estimation-and-tracking-from-ultrasound-tongue-images/) (2020). Formant target and hybrid front end (M06) are standard; evaluation (M12) is behind: random one-second clip split of one speaker, where earlier ultrasound work held out whole utterances.
- [Vocoder-Based Speech Synthesis from Silent Videos](vocoder-based-speech-synthesis-from-silent-videos/) (2020). WORLD features (M06) beat the 2019 GAN on seen GRID speakers; on unseen ones PESQ ties and ESTOI is higher. The CTC text loss (M02) adds under 0.015 ESTOI; the WER is the text head's.
- [Autoencoder-Based Articulatory-to-Acoustic Mapping for Ultrasound Silent Speech Interfaces](autoencoder-based-articulatory-to-acoustic-mapping-for-ultrasound-silent-speech-interfaces/) (2019). Standard on the encoder: autoencoder features predate it and it is never compared with PCA or DCT; behind on F0, which is copied from the recording, so the listening test is not of a silent system.
- [Speech synthesis from neural decoding of spoken sentences](speech-synthesis-from-neural-decoding-of-spoken-sentences/) (2019). Listener-tested sentence synthesis from ECoG, alongside concurrent work; its articulatory-intermediate gain is not isolated from extra supervision, and intelligibility rests on closed word pools.
- [Ultrasound-based Silent Speech Interface Built on a Continuous Vocoder](ultrasound-based-silent-speech-interface-built-on-a-continuous-vocoder/) (2019). Continuous F0 targets from articulation were prior work; MVF carrying voicing instead of a flag is new but unproven: the F0 RMSE gain uses different reference contours and naturalness is not better.
- [Video-Driven Speech Reconstruction using Generative Adversarial Networks](video-driven-speech-reconstruction-using-generative-adversarial-networks/) (2019). Direct-waveform GAN (M06) and speech-encoder perceptual loss (M02) are early but standard; the perceptual loss carries the words. Unseen-speaker WER 40.5% (successor 16%); no listener test.
- [Deep Audio-Visual Speech Recognition](deep-audio-visual-speech-recognition/) (2018). LRS2-BBC outlasts the models: the CTC vs seq2seq comparison is confounded, the 22-point gain is not attributable to the new architecture, and test speaker overlap is unreported.
- [Harnessing AI for Speech Reconstruction using Multi-view Silent Video Feed](harnessing-ai-for-speech-reconstruction-using-multi-view-silent-video-feed/) (2018). Multi-view input (M05) is new for video-to-speech but standard on its evidence: 2 speakers, gains overstated (11.4% in Table 1, 26% in text); PESQ only, no listener test (M12).
- [Lip2AudSpec: Speech Reconstruction from Silent Lip Movements Video](lip2audspec-speech-reconstruction-from-silent-lip-movements-video/) (2018). Bottleneck target (M02) and auditory-spectrogram route (M06) are standard; gains rest on one speaker, a re-implemented baseline and a vocabulary-cued test on four seen GRID speakers.
- [Updating the Silent Speech Challenge benchmark with deep learning](updating-the-silent-speech-challenge-benchmark-with-deep-learning/) (2018). Kaldi DNN-HMM cuts SSC WER from 17.4% to 6.45% (M06, M12: standard), but with the same bigram its own GMM gives 12.71%, so the DNN halves WER; one speaker, single runs.
- [Visual-Only Recognition of Normal, Whispered and Silent Speech](visual-only-recognition-of-normal-whispered-and-silent-speech/) (2018). Ahead on data: the first public three-mode lip-video corpus. Standard on transfer: the silent drop was known, and most of it is test-mode difficulty, not the training mode.
- [Improved Speech Reconstruction from Silent Video](improved-speech-reconstruction-from-silent-video/) (2017). Optical-flow tower (M05) and mel-to-linear post-net (M06) are standard: no flow gain in Table 5; trails Le Cornu on S3; no listener test (M12); Lip2Wav's re-run: 27.83% vs 14.08% WER.
- [Lip Reading Sentences in the Wild](lip-reading-sentences-in-the-wild/) (2017). Made sentence lipreading measurable with LRS, but its decoder and curriculum gains are confounded with data and augmentation, and the beats-a-lipreader claim is on unequal terms.
