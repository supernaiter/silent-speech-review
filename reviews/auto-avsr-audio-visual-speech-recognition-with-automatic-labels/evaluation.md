## Required fixes (ordered by impact)

1. **M12 and Verdict state of the art (rows 18, 19).** Replace "with public data, USR 2.0 … 17.6% and 0.8%" as follows. Public-data VSR best: SynthVSR [6], 16.9% (18.2% without LM), 2023, concurrent. AVSR: Llama-AVSR (2024, arXiv:2409.12319) at 0.77%, and Whisper-Flamingo (2024) at 0.8%, are later and better or equal. Then either define "public data" (it excludes Whisper-initialised models, and then also Auto-AVSR's Whisper or CM-T labels) or drop the qualifier. Recompute "trails today's best by 0.1 point".
2. **Single-run claim (row 4).** "Reports every row from a single run with no seeds" is not in §3.3. Change it to "reports no run count, seeds or spread" in M12, the M03 and M12 table cells, and the Verdict.
3. **Scale effect from 31.5 to 19.1 (row 13).** State that the target attributes 1.7 points at equal data to model capacity (§4.2: 34.7 in [3] against 33.0 at 818 h). State also that 31.5 is best-of-ten while 19.1 is unrepeated. The same-model data effect is 33.0 to 19.1 (Table 2), which the Verdict already uses; keep that figure as the scale effect.
4. **Afouras et al. condition and the distillation contrast (rows 11, 14).** 59.8% needs ground-truth fine-tuning; with ASR labels only, LRS3 WER is 61.7%. Flag that the target's §1 describes [9] as distillation only, although [9] also trained with CTC on ASR transcripts. Do not say the sentence "separates it from Afouras et al.".
5. **30% headline (rows 3, 25).** Add the 0.9 against 1.3 reconstruction (31%, inference, AV-HuBERT audio-only row). Tie the weakness of the headline to its unnamed baseline, not to missing repetition.
6. **M02 usual-approach cell (row 21).** "Human transcripts only" contradicts the text and the tallies. Match the cell to the text and say which count the 18 (up to 2022-09) refers to.
7. **Label WER request (row 23).** Credit Table 1's LRS3 labeller WER, and ask for label WER on the added corpora.
8. **Code-default question (row 24).** Give the repository file and commit, or rephrase it as a question without the factual premise.
9. **Minor.** Add the Table 4 "Ours 1 902" quote to claim row 24 and note that 1,902 is the arithmetic sum. Fix the [5] venue (ICASSP 2024, pp. 10136–10140). Cite [3, 16] rather than [5] for the target's §1 contrast. Mention that USR 2.0 uses no LM while Auto-AVSR's VSR does. Add an evaluation-practice baseline to the M12 usual-approach line. Note that Whisper against CM-T (19.0 against 19.1) sits inside the review's own noise band.

## Scores

- Procedure: 6/10. The claim table is complete and the quotes are mostly exact. But a source the reader extracted (Llama-AVSR) was dropped, SynthVSR's own SOTA statement was missed, and a "single run" claim rests on a quote that does not say it.
- Final text: 6/10. The target-paper criticisms in "Where the claims outrun the evidence" are accurate. The state-of-the-art lines and the scale-effect attribution need correction.

Strengths (brief): the internal-inconsistency catches are correct and well located (1,921 against 1,902 h; the missing CM-aux 31.5 row; the 26× claim holding only for AVSR; babble overlap). The lineage link to [3]'s ten-run means is a fair and specific anchor.
