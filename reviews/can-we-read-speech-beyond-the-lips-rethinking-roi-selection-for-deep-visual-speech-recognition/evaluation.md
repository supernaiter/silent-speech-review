## Required fixes, by impact

1. **M05 absence claim and verdict (#12, #2).** Replace "No prior decoder in the population feeds the whole face" and "Whole face as decoder input: 0 of 162" with the named priors. Cox et al. 2008 (AVSP, full-face vs mouth-only AAMs, mouth better) and Neti et al. 2000 (JSALT final report, whole-face AAMs, "limited success") are same-task face-versus-mouth priors, alongside the cited Chung and Zisserman profile model and Vid2speech. The "ahead" verdict can stand only for isolating extraoral regions, which is an analysis probe rather than the paper's chosen input. It must also state its repetition honestly: one independent dataset (LRW), LRW-1000 initialised from the LRW front end, and GRID decoded with an LM and no chance WER. Under the rule (repetition across datasets, deciding priors read), I would downgrade M05 to `standard`. The aligned face input has priors and does not beat the mouth on LRW, and the novelty (the probe) belongs in the text as a contribution, not as a sensor verdict. If "ahead" is kept, name Cox 2008 and Neti 2000 as the deciding priors and give the repetition as above.
2. **Population dedupe (#13).** Split the 34-id merged row (`p:arxiv-1611.06986`), re-date its members (Auto-AVSR 2023, Sterpu 2020 and app13116521 are not prior), drop the perception thesis, re-run the hand checks over the split members (Cox 2008 is among them), and update 505 / 162 / 128 / 0-of-162 / 26 everywhere. Remove "every prior and [concurrent] title was checked by hand" until that is true.
3. **M03 verdict (#2, #5).** No counterexample was found, so a first use in lip reading is plausible. But the "repeated over three datasets, one run each" repetition is weaker than stated. The LRW-1000 Cutout model starts from the LRW Cutout front end, and the GRID gain is 0.2 WER points with an LM. Without a mouth-plus-Cutout control, the gain cannot be attributed to extraoral cues. I accept "ahead" as borderline only if the text states repetition = LRW plus GRID (0.2 points) and notes the LRW-1000 dependence. Otherwise use `standard` with the first use credited in prose.
4. **SyncVSR conditions (#17).** Replace "LRW only" with "LRW video, audio-token targets from an externally pretrained vq-wav2vec, 128×128 mouth-area crop". Say this line does not bear on the face RoI.
5. **"Largest gain" (#6).** The LRW-1000 gain over the mouth (+6.60) is larger. Reword.
6. **Like-for-like state of the art (#18, #21, #23).** For Zhang 2022 use 34.6 vs 40.3 (same iteration) or state the extra iteration. For Ma 2022 cite 90.4% (no WB, no distillation) as the comparable figure, or keep 92.8 and state WB plus self-distillation. State "without word boundaries" for the 84.07 comparison and name Stafylakis 2018 at 88.8% with WB. Mention concurrent Martinez 2020 at 85.3%, labelled [concurrent].
7. **GRID (#24).** Add LCANet's abstract value of 3.0% and LRSW's 3.0%. Say the target's figure uses an LM that the comparators do not state.
8. **Wording fixes (#10, #11, #19, #25, #3).** Say Vid2speech's error is a regression error. Fix the Koumparoulis base (27% reduction from LHF). Add "30 h labelled" to the masking result. Replace "uninterpretable" with "within about two seed SDs of another system (inference)". Write "negative on LRW when aligned" in M05.
9. **Baseline-strength confound (#22).** Consider one sentence noting the same group's 46.5% lip baseline on LRW-1000 (Feng Table 3), with comparability marked unverified.
10. **References.** Cite the target as IEEE FG 2020, pp. 356–363, doi:10.1109/FG47880.2020.00134 (verified via Crossref), not only the arXiv DOI. Venue versions exist for several others (Vid2speech ICASSP 2017, LCANet FG 2018, Weng & Kitani BMVC 2019, LRW-1000 FG 2019, Ma 2022 ICASSP 2022, SyncVSR Interspeech 2024; unverified here). The phrase "best LRW result read" reads as process narration; "best LRW result" is enough.
