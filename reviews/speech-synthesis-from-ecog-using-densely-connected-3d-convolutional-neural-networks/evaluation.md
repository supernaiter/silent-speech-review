## Required fixes, ordered by impact

1. **Component table counting basis (row 25, high).** The slot-filled numbers for M01–M05, M07, M09–M11 and M13 count studies with a filled slot across the 7,388-study wiki, not studies using the named design (for example, 7,329 "clinical ECoG grid" studies is impossible). Either show the real design count with its denominator and tier, or replace the number with "–". Give M13 a design label (for example "offline analysis"), not "Hardware and cost".
2. **Misattributed protocol (row 23, high).** Berezutskaya [4] did not use word-held-out folds: every test word also appeared in training. Rewrite the M12 "needs next" item, citing Chen [2] (Fig. 2b, unique-word folds) for word-held-out testing and [4] only for identification plus listener tests.
3. **Kohler confound (row 11, medium-high).** State that Kohler's DenseNet received raw sEEG time series rather than the target's broadband-gamma features, on Dutch sentences with a fixed split. The "beat it" claim in the Verdict and in M06 should carry that condition.
4. **Berezutskaya correlation result (row 12, medium).** The DN correlation lead over S2S was statistically significant (H = 14.26), and the authors called it marginal in size. The DN was also re-optimized with Optuna. Say "significant but small", not "only marginally ahead".
5. **Griffin-Lim inference (row 16, medium).** Delete or rewrite "so the neural vocoder does not set the state of the art". Chen's spectrogram PCC is independent of the waveform step. If a waveform-level comparison is wanted, use [6] or a listener or STOI measure.
6. **"The design proved well chosen" (row 10, medium).** This works as an informal "ahead" judgement for M06, backed by one later study that never tested DenseNet and is contradicted by the review's own head-to-heads and by [6] Table I (3D-CNN 5.04 vs FC-DNN 4.55 dB MCD). Soften it to "the 3D spatio-temporal treatment of the grid recurs in later decoders [2]". Consider adding the [6] 3D-CNN vs FC-DNN result as a later test of the small-data claim.
7. **"most natural" (row 3, low).** This is a design rationale in the Abstract, not a reported result. Either drop it from the list of unsupported claims or describe it as an unmeasured design intent.
8. **STOI chance level (row 21, low).** Note that the STOI null is a single 95th-percentile randomized spectrogram, unlike the 60-run correlation null.
9. **Validation question (row 24, low).** Acknowledge the fixed 80 epochs (§2.4) in the question.
10. **"Read in full: [4], [5]" for M12 (low).** In the M12 audit, these `read_fulltext` entries are inherited from another review (`by: relating-the-neural-representations-…`). [5] has no reader text in this work folder. The quote does match the wiki source, so this is an issue of provenance labelling, not of accuracy.
