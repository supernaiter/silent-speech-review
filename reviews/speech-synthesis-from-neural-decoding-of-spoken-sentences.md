# Speech synthesis from neural decoding of spoken sentences

**This review has moved to [reviews/speech-synthesis-from-neural-decoding-of-spoken-sentences/](speech-synthesis-from-neural-decoding-of-spoken-sentences/).**

Two-stage bLSTM decoder maps ECoG during reading aloud to inferred articulatory features and then to acoustic parameters, synthesizing sentence waveforms (median MCD 5.14-6.58 dB in five participants). The two-stage model beat a direct decoder by 0.54 dB in P1, but the intermediate targets add phonetic-label supervision and the decoders differ in depth and learning rate; listener transcription used closed 25/50-word pools.
