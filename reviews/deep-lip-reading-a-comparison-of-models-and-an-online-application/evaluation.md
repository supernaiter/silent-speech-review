## Required fixes, by impact

### Major
1. **M06, BL vs FC-10 (row 11).** Drop "but FC-10 uses CTC and beats BL by 5.1 points" as a rebuttal. Credit the authors' hedge ("we suspect … in part", Sec. 3.3). State instead that the explanation is untested (no BL with an attention decoder) and does not explain why BL is worse rather than merely no better.
2. **M13 basis and count (rows 13, 19).**
   - Replace "5 of 12" in the prose, the table and slots.json (n = 12, matched = 5) with the count at the target's time: 1 of 4 prior, with a note on the possible Cai duplicate. If the later work is kept, give it separately (4 of 8 later).
   - Recast "Not reported". The target does give an algorithmic lag (22/32 frames, about 0.88/1.28 s at 25 fps, the same kind of figure as [3]'s 1,060 ms), and its final online output equals the offline WER (Sec. 4).
   - Say what is actually missing: partial-hypothesis accuracy, delay in ms, and timing of the W = 100 beam search with r extra steps per frame. Drop "that [3] measures".
3. **M06, the strongest confound (rows 3, 9).** In the target, the CTC-versus-attention comparison is also a BLSTM/FC-versus-Transformer comparison, so objective and architecture are confounded. The review lists beam and LM confounds but not this one. Cite the [concurrent] [8] Table 2: TM-CTC 54.7% vs TM-seq2seq 48.3% with the external LM, on one Transformer encoder. Also qualify the "family gap" with CER: FC-15 33.9% vs TM 34.0% (T2), and greedy 35.3% vs 38.6% (Table 1).
4. **Baseline data confound (row 24).** Rephrase "Where the claims outrun the evidence", bullet 2: Auto-AVSR lists MV-WAS as LRS2-only (223 h), but the target says MV-LRS was used in the earlier work (Sec. 3.1), so the baseline's training data are unresolved. Do not assert that the gap mixes data and model.

### Moderate
5. **"For the first time" (row 8).** Attribute the phrase correctly: it is about repurposing MT/ASR architectures for lip reading (Sec. 1). The 0-prior CTC-versus-attention result is the review's own ledger finding and should be stated as such.
6. **5-point threshold (row 3).** Mark it "(inference)" with its basis, or drop it: no source gives run-to-run spread.
7. **Missing prior from the target's reference list (row 2).** Name the target's ref. [15], MV-WAS (Chung and Zisserman, BMVC 2017), as a prior attention lip reader missing from the ledger. Note Stafylakis 2017's word-level temporal-convolution back end (target Sec. 3.2).
8. **Verdict, "over 20" (row 4).** Add "as points"; read as relative change (27%), the claim holds with T1.
9. **T2 gain for TM (rows 10, 22).** For TM, the T1 column has no external LM (App. A.3: α = 0.0, W = 5). Its 1.2-point gain is LM plus wider beam versus none, not a corpus effect.

### Minor
10. **Table 5 (row 17).** It shows one utterance from two starting points, not two examples.
11. **Frame rate (row 16).** Cite [8] Sec. 4 for LRS2's 25 fps.
12. **Conditions to add (rows 14, 18, 20).** Sterpu: viseme accuracy, 12 classes, TCD-TIMIT. Ma 2023: LRS3-only, 438 h, algorithmic delay. Cai: Julius, CPU time / audio time.
13. **Appendix locators.** App. A.3/A.4, Algorithm 1 and Tables 3 and 5 exist only in arXiv v1. Mark them "arXiv v1 appendix", since the DOI version has none.
14. **Population label (row 1).** It includes phrase classification, viseme classification and ultrasound SSI. Relabel it "sequence-level visual and audio-visual speech decoders" or note the inclusion.
15. **Reference [10].** It appeared at IEEE ICIP 2018 (venue from memory; DOI unverified). Also remove the doubled punctuation "?.".
16. **M06 ctc-vs-attention Appendix line.** "Recall estimate 1.0" next to "known-item recall 0.5" reads as a contradiction; one clause of explanation would help. Also say that the hybrid exclusion removes the [concurrent] LCANet (arXiv 1803.04988, attention-CTC) and Sterpu's joint CTC-Seq2seq row. Neither changes the prior count of 0.

Unverified, so no fix is required: a later LRS2 visual-only WER below Auto-AVSR's 14.6%, and a later streaming visual-only LRS3 result that beats Ma et al. 2023.
