## Required fixes, ordered by impact

1. **High. Mention counts shown as design counts.** Location: Verdict sentence 2 ("spellers at the time used evoked potentials or motor and cursor selection (191 each of 252 studies ...)"); M04, M07, M09, M12 "Usual approach" lines ("Of 252 spelling studies ... 191 name ..."); 13-components table cells "(pattern match 191/39/71/67)"; `slots.json` `matched` 191/39/71/67. Evidence: claim 10 (Willett 2021 counted under four M04 designs; a review and a post-target EEG dataset counted as evoked-potential spellers; non-spelling papers in the 252). Fix: in `slots.json` set `matched: null` for M04, M07, M09, M12 and put the counts in `note` as "mentions"; in the text write "of 252 studies whose full text matches the spelling pattern, 191 mention an evoked-potential speller (pattern match, mentions and reviews count, Appendix)"; delete "spellers at the time used ..." and "252 spelling studies"; in the Verdict state only that no study in the set mentions NATO code words for spelling.
2. **High. M04 reason and Verdict logic.** Location: Verdict "All three are standard: ... and silently mouthed NATO-word sequences had been decoded from sEMG [2]"; M04 "Across signal types the design had precedents". Evidence: claim 12; the Appendix's own NATO check says "none a speller". The target does not match the usual approach (it uses neither evoked potentials nor motor selection). Fix: give M04 as standard because the difference is shown in one participant only (SKILL rule "not supported well enough to call"), with 0 of 252 and no speller among the NATO-word works (Appendix), and keep Meltzner as "NATO words decoded silently as vocabulary items, not as a letter code". Using cross-signal works is consistent with the Appendix setting (any signal type), but a precedent must have the same design.
3. **Medium. Time cutoff.** Location: Appendix "252 dated 2022 or earlier ... (target first public 2022-11)"; all "at the target's time" counts. Evidence: claim 11. Fix: date the cutoff at 2022-11-08 (received 2021-12-22; no preprint found), count prior as first public before 2022-05-08, report 2022-05 to 2023-05 as [concurrent] separately, and drop `biorxiv-2022.11.15.516461` from the prior set; or relabel the column "dated 2022 or earlier by index year, including [concurrent] and some later works". State the 48 undated studies and that SilentSpeller is among them.
4. **Medium. Comparator conditions.** Location: Verdict "about a third of attempted handwriting"; M04 State of the art "90 characters per minute at 5.4% character error rate" and "110 ... about 3.7 times the target". Evidence: claims 14, 16. Fix: "90 characters per minute at 5.4% raw online character error rate without a language model (0.89% with an offline language model) [3]"; "peak 110 characters per minute (participant T18, one session; T17 peak 47) at 1.6% word error rate [6]"; compare peak with the target's best block (30.79, about 3.6 times, inference) or median with median. Note that the authors themselves make the 90 CPM comparison (Discussion).
5. **Medium. "First" claim left untested, written as a process disclaimer.** Location: M09 "the Appendix counts do not test it". Fix: intersect speech-decoding studies with attempted hand-command studies among the prior set and read the few hits (snippets), then either report "none of the N ... combines speech-motor and hand-motor control (pattern match, Appendix)" or name the earlier work. If not done, drop the quote and the disclaimer sentence; leaving it as "credited but not tested" is not acceptable in the review text.
