# Lip Reading Sentences in the Wild

**This review has moved to [reviews/lip-reading-sentences-in-the-wild/](lip-reading-sentences-in-the-wild/).**

Introduces Watch, Listen, Attend and Spell and the BBC LRS dataset. Lips-only decoding reaches 50.2% WER on open-vocabulary broadcast sentences against 73.8% for a professional lipreader, but the lipreader worked on 200 clips under different viewing conditions with declined clips excluded (87.6% with them), and the machine score is not stated to be on the same clips. The word-to-sentence curriculum gives the largest single gain (76.5% to 61.1%), from one training run.
